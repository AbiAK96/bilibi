A workspace tile has no title bar: the page's own heading names it. Its controls float over the top right corner, a grip for dragging the tile onto another to swap them and a menu with the two operations a pointer needs. A focused tile keeps them in sight; on any other tile they appear when the pointer is over it or focus reaches them, so hover the second example. The menu closes when focus leaves it or Escape is pressed. Every other tile operation is a key of the tiling mode.
